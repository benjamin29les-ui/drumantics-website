Our Story

OUR BEGINNINGS

A Passion That Started With One Student

Drumantics & Music began more than 12 years ago with one student and a simple hope: to share the joy of music. Founded by musician and educator Carlo and his wife, our school has grown from humble beginnings into a welcoming community where students of all ages can learn, grow and enjoy making music.

One student.
One passion.
A growing community.

Sharing the joy of music, one student at a time.

THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND THE MUSIC

Meet Our Founder

Carlo brings years of professional performance experience. He has performed in the United States, Abu Dhabi, Japan and across Asia, including as a cruise drummer aboard Disney Cruise Line’s Disney Magic.

He was also a two-time finalist in Malaysia’s Roland V-Drums competition.

LEARNING & GROWING

Music Education For Every Journey

We offer lessons in a range of instruments for students who want to learn for enjoyment and for those working towards graded music examinations.

Students can prepare for Rockschool (RSL), ABRSM and Trinity examinations, from beginner level through to diploma.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

More Than Just Music Lessons

At Drumantics, our team believes music is more than a skill to master. It can bring joy, offer comfort to the soul and connect people.

We teach with passion and care, encouraging every student to build confidence, progress at their own pace and find their own connection with music.

OUR MISSION

Making Every Musical Journey Meaningful

Our mission is to make learning music a meaningful and joyful journey, and to create a community where that love of music can grow.
